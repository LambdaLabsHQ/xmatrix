# xMatrix 4.3.0

This update is the website. The CLI/daemon stays on 4.2.1, the Desktop app on
4.0.0, the Android app on 4.1.0 (still in Google Play review), and iOS on
4.0.4. Hub changes are already live.

## Subscriptions in the iPhone and iPad app

Settings → Billing in the iOS app now names each subscription and its length
next to its price, for example `xMatrix Pro Monthly · 1 month · $10.00/month`,
and links Apple's standard Terms of Use (EULA) beside the Privacy Policy and
Terms of Service. The Android app's buttons show the same title and length.
The App Store listing links the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy again.
Prices, seats and how a subscription is bound to its Space are unchanged.

## Signing in to the apps

When the Desktop or phone app signs in and your browser is already signed in to
xMatrix, the app uses that web session instead of sending you through Google or
GitHub again. Choosing Google or GitHub still opens that provider, so you can
pick a different account, and signing out does not sign the app straight back
in. If the browser is not signed in, the app shows its usual sign-in buttons.

## Schedules are now programs

The Schedules view is gone from the rail, More, search, Status and Space
settings; schedule lines (`🔁`/`⏳` task lines) no longer run and now show as
ordinary task lines. Recurring work, reminders and event-driven work are Norn
programs: a `norn run` block on a page. The user guide has a new Programs page
(<https://xmatrix.sh/docs/programs>) covering a first program, requests and
replies, routines and reminders, and event streams. An old Schedules link opens
Pages.

## Known limitations

- The CLI command `xmatrix page automation` is removed only in the next
  CLI/daemon release; with 4.2.1 it reaches an endpoint that no longer runs
  anything.
- iOS 4.0.4 is back in App Review; it is not yet on the App Store.
