# xMatrix 4.6.2

This update is the Android app, with a security fix. The website and the
CLI/daemon stay on 4.6.1, the Desktop app on 4.0.0, and iOS on 4.0.4. Hub
changes listed below are already live.

## Fixes

### Android app

- Other apps installed on your phone can no longer tell xMatrix which page to
  open. Before, an installed app could send the xMatrix app an address, open
  any page of the xMatrix website inside it, or open the signed-in login page
  with a device code of its own.
- Links from outside the app still work where they are meant to: a link to a
  conversation opens that conversation, and returning from sign-in completes
  the sign-in. Any other link opens the app as usual.
- Tapping the home-screen icon of a running app brings it back as you left it.
  Existing home-screen shortcuts keep working.

### Conversations

- If you summon an Agent before connecting a computer, the conversation now
  explains how to connect one. Before, it showed a launch failure with nothing
  to act on.
- Fixed page embeds of GitHub files failing, and Android push notifications
  being dropped, when several requests arrived at once.

### Connectors

- The App Store Connect connector no longer submits In-App Purchases when Apple
  has more of them than it can read. It refuses and submits nothing, and its
  list says when it is incomplete.

## Compatibility

No action is needed. The fix reaches Android users when they update the app.
Google Play makes this version available after its review.
