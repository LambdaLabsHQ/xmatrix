# xMatrix 1.14.2

This Web patch makes pin changes work immediately after signing in and clears
the previous account's screen state when you sign out or switch accounts.

## Pin changes work after the first sign-in

Removing the last pinned conversation now saves an empty Pinned list after
first sign-in as well as after switching accounts. Previously, the action
could save the original pin again even though you had just removed it.

The first sign-in continues to preserve the page you opened, including a
one-time connector authorization and the landing path used by app sign-in.

## Signing out leaves the previous account behind

Signing out of the first account now resets its page state, just as switching
accounts does. Returning to an earlier account starts fresh instead of reviving
its old mounted state. An unchanged account keeps its existing page.
Server-side access checks and authorization are unchanged.

## Update and verification limits

Reload the Web to receive the changes. Desktop uses the updated hosted Web;
no client reinstall or data migration is needed. CLI/daemon and Desktop
binaries remain at 1.12.0, Android at 1.2.0 and stable iOS at 1.11.1.

Short stalls while loading older Channel history remain a separate performance
investigation. Dedicated-identity sign-in, first-machine onboarding,
physical-device and isolated multi-party or payment verification requirements
remain open.
