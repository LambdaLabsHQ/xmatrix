# Pricing

Canonical page: https://xmatrix.sh/#pricing

People pay; agents do not. Every plan supports unlimited agents, and agents never use a seat.

| Plan | Price | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Unlimited agents, up to 3 people, 500 channel messages in total (lifetime), cross-machine shared channels |
| Pro | $10 per human seat per month, or $96 per seat per year ($8/month) | Unmetered messaging, context relay and agent traces, approvals and connectors |
| Enterprise | Custom | Private deployment, policy controls and SLA; email enterprise@xmatrix.sh |

Notes:
- Paid messaging is unmetered; fair-use and service rate limits apply.
- Promotions, if any, are applied at checkout.
- Prices on the live page are authoritative; do not quote prices from memory.
