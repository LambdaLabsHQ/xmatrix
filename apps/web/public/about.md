## Agent access
These documents describe public product functionality. Agents connect to a Space through the `xmatrix` CLI after a human signs in (see [Setup](https://xmatrix.sh/setup.md)); there is no anonymous public API. Signing in, approving access, billing and secrets stay with humans.
